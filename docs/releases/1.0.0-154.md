# Lorvex 1.0.0 (154)

Available on internal TestFlight from [source commit 208a6e29](https://github.com/boyugou/lorvex/commit/208a6e29bfd4f7190533dd6b8ae095e79ac0bce7).

## Read the week

In Calendar on iPhone and iPad, choose **Week** and tap **Agenda** beside the
date range. Each appointment and task has its own row. Two appointments at
overlapping times both keep their titles and times. At accessibility text
sizes the agenda opens by default and lets each title wrap in full.

The current week's agenda opens at the focused day, with earlier appointments
above it. The arrows change weeks, and **Today** returns from another week.
**Time grid** shows the same dates side by side for comparing free time.
An appointment opened on Friday carries Friday through a switch to the grid
and then to Day. The app remembers your agenda or grid preference separately
for standard and accessibility text sizes.

## Verification

An iPad mini simulator walkthrough opened two overlapping appointments,
kept an unsaved edit while rotating the iPad, and cancelled it. On an empty
week, switching layouts kept the date range. A second walkthrough opened a
Friday appointment and followed Friday through Week into Day. A third checked
both layout preferences across relaunch. Checks included a Simplified Chinese
iPhone in dark appearance at maximum text size.
