# Lorvex 1.0.0 (149)

Available on internal TestFlight from [source commit 7a1aab74](https://github.com/boyugou/lorvex/commit/7a1aab749941ecb48c097989cbbee04b17465de8).

## Weekly journal

Open Review and switch to Weekly to read the days you wrote about, most
recent first. A day can contain a summary, wins, blockers, learnings or
just ratings. Select a date to open its full entry. With several entries,
use the menu beside the heading to read one topic across the week.

Choose **Plan a next step** beside a blocker. Give the action a name and
choose its planned day. The date starts on the upcoming Monday. **Add a
task** saves the task with the chosen blocker's words and the date of the
original entry in its notes. The task opens from the confirmation beside
the blocker and appears on the chosen day in Calendar. A date within the
next seven days also places it in the current review's week-ahead agenda.

## Verification

An isolated Mac preview opened a blocker-only entry in the weekly digest,
filtered the journal, scheduled a task and opened its inspector. One run
kept the suggested Monday and one chose Wednesday. The iPhone simulator
exercised the topic menu, composer, empty-title guard and cancellation.
The Simplified Chinese journal was checked at the largest accessibility
text size. Tests covered a historical journal with multiple blockers, an
unedited source entry, year and daylight-saving boundaries, literal task
titles and calendar refresh on iPhone.

## Distribution

The signed iOS and macOS packages passed Apple validation. App Store
Connect reported **VALID** and **IN_BETA_TESTING** on both platforms.
Both builds belong to their existing internal tester groups and expire
on **2027-02-18**. English and Simplified Chinese test notes were saved
and read back for each build.
