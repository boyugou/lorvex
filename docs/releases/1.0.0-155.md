## Keep a name intact during capture

Type **Watch The Day After Tomorrow** into quick capture. The preview shows
that “Tomorrow” would set a date. Select **Keep title as typed** to save the
movie name in full. The choice applies to this entry. The next time you type
**Call the cinema tomorrow**, the preview and saved task use tomorrow again.

On Mac the choice is available in Today, Tasks, a list, the menu bar and the
floating capture window. The command palette offers the original title as a
second result. A task entered in Today still lands on Today, and a task
entered inside a list stays in that list.

On iPhone and iPad, tap the row beneath the title before adding the task.
In a multi-line capture the choice applies to all lines. Each nonempty line
becomes one task and keeps the shared notes.

## Verification and distribution

An iPhone 17 Pro simulator walkthrough saved the movie name, then the dated
call. After relaunch the stored movie had its full title and no planned day
or deadline. The call retained its original input and had the next day's plan. At the largest
accessibility text size, a Simplified Chinese walkthrough scrolled to the
choice and selected it in dark appearance with the keyboard open.
Mac checks used an isolated task database and covered Today, list, global
capture and the command palette. Both light and dark floating capture views
were rendered. The full Swift suites, static gates and localization checks passed.

Apple validated the signed iOS and macOS packages. Both builds reported
**VALID** and **IN_BETA_TESTING** in the existing internal groups. English and
Simplified Chinese testing notes were read back on each. The builds expire
on **2027-02-19**.

Apple accepted notarization of the direct Mac app and disk image. Gatekeeper
accepted the stapled download. The public DMG and checksum were downloaded
anonymously and matched the local package. This verification covered the
download and signature. The packaged app was not installed or launched on
the production Mac profile.
