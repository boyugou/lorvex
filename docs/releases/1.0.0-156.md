## Find the task you just saved

Add an undated task from Today on iPhone or iPad. The capture sheet closes
and the task title and list appear above the tab bar. Tap **Open** to read
or edit the task. Going back returns to Today. Ignoring the confirmation
keeps the current page in place.

Paste several lines into capture and tap **Open** to see the tasks from that
submission. Each row shows its saved title and list. Open a row to see its
details, then go back to the batch. A normal confirmation clears after eight
seconds. With VoiceOver, focus moves to the title and destination, followed
by **Open**, and the confirmation stays available.

## Verification

The iPhone 17 Pro simulator on iOS 26.5 covered an undated Inbox task, a batch
spanning Work and Inbox, and opening a task at the largest Simplified Chinese
text size. The iPad mini simulator also opened a task from a mixed-list batch
and navigated back. An iOS 27 VoiceOver run read the title and Inbox destination
after the sheet closed, read the Open button next, and opened the task by
double-tap after the ordinary timeout. These walkthroughs used isolated
simulators. The same VoiceOver sequence is covered by the repository's
**LorvexMobileUITests** scheme. Full Swift and core suites, static gates and
localization checks passed.
