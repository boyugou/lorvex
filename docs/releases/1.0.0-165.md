# Lorvex 1.0.0 (165)

Available on internal TestFlight for iPhone, iPad and Mac. [Build source](https://github.com/boyugou/lorvex/commit/1ea58a633301cdc959d75abd84eb6d69d8b30708). The [direct Mac download](https://github.com/boyugou/lorvex/releases/tag/v1.0.0%2B164) is build 164.

## Choosing tomorrow’s tasks on iPhone and iPad

In **Review → Day → Still open**, tap **Plan Tomorrow**. The sheet shows the
remaining time in your working day and updates it as you select tasks. Expand
**Already planned** to see the events and work behind the estimate. Tasks with
unknown durations have a separate count.

![Tomorrow’s existing plans, one selected task, and thirty minutes remaining on iPhone in Simplified Chinese](../images/iphone-review-plan-tomorrow-165-zh.png)

**Move to Tomorrow** shows how many tasks you selected. Cancel discards the
selection. After confirming, tap **Undo** in the banner to restore the previous
plans. At accessibility text sizes the forecast scrolls with the list, leaving
room for the task titles and the confirmation button.

## Verification

The native iPhone 17 Pro and iPad mini simulator walkthroughs checked the
default working hours and a shorter 9 am–noon window. With an hour-long call
and two proposed tasks of 45 and 90 minutes, the short day ran 15 minutes
over. Leaving the 45-minute task behind showed 30 minutes free. Both devices
also showed the existing grocery task with its duration unknown.

Both walkthroughs checked cancellation, an empty selection, a one-task move,
Undo, and task selection at the largest Dynamic Type size. Reading back the
simulator databases confirmed the original task dates, times and deadline.
The Chinese walkthrough repeated the forecast change and expanded the agenda
on both devices, including iPhone in dark appearance.

The full Swift and static suites passed. The signed iOS and Mac packages
contain the planning copy in 30 languages. Both builds were processed and
assigned to the existing internal TestFlight groups, with English and
Simplified Chinese testing notes. They expire on **2027-02-19**.
