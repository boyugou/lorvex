## Task dates

Task lists show the day and time you set aside for work. A separate deadline
is labeled **Due**. A day shared by the work and its deadline appears once.
The Mac table keeps the work day under the title and the deadline in its
Due column. Task search results and list previews on Mac also show the plan.

On iPhone and iPad, **Tasks → Scheduled** includes open and in-progress tasks
with a plan or a deadline. A task created from a Weekly Review blocker
appears in Scheduled on the strength of its work date alone. Collection
counts and searches cover the full matching set, including tasks beyond the
first page.

## Verification

An iPhone simulator created a task from a weekly blocker, kept its suggested
Monday, found it in Scheduled and opened its details. Mac previews covered
both the task queue and the table. An iPad simulator showed the collection
beside the selected task. Simplified Chinese was checked on Mac and mobile,
including the largest accessibility text size on iPhone.

The app and core test suites and static release checks passed. Regression
tests searched English and Chinese titles among more than 200 undated
tasks, checked counts across pages, and distinguished a visibility date
from a work day or deadline. Date label tests covered two time zones
around the spring clock change.
