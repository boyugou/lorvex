# Lorvex 1.0.0 (151)

Available on internal TestFlight from [source commit 5c1c0fe3](https://github.com/boyugou/lorvex/commit/5c1c0fe3c0abf7ffff86372876e412bc67e4f43e).

## Reading an earlier day

Select a date in Weekly Review to reread that day. On iPhone and iPad the
entry opens with the original note, wins, blockers and learnings, including
entries with just one of those fields. Written sections are selectable.
Mood and energy appear as the words recorded that day. An empty day shows
a short message above any activity. **Return to today** opens today's editor.

Mac uses the same reader when you select an earlier date in Review.

## Verification

The iPhone simulator opened yesterday from the weekly journal, read a
blocker with no summary, returned to today, typed a note and read it back
after relaunch. Simplified Chinese captures covered a blocker, a ratings-only
entry and an empty day. An iPad simulator displayed the blocker and its
ratings in dark appearance at the largest accessibility text size. Regression
tests cover each individual text field, each rating and whitespace-only text.
