Available on internal TestFlight for iPhone, iPad and Mac. [Build source](https://github.com/boyugou/lorvex/commit/8b84c33bb1e7d49a4b4bf564d31cdc1a4524c6eb). The [direct Mac download](https://github.com/boyugou/lorvex/releases/latest) at the time of this build is [158](1.0.0-158.md).

## Feeling and energy in the mobile journal

Open **Review** on iPhone or iPad. The two controls below your note show the
day’s feeling and energy. Tap one to choose a word. Its menu offers **Clear**
once it has a value. The controls sit side by side at standard text sizes and
stack at xxLarge and above. Each keeps a touch target of at least 44 points.

A second rating can arrive while the first is still saving. Mobile Review now
waits for the pending write before saving the next edit. Changing days or
backgrounding the app finishes edits on the reviewed day.

## Verification

An iPhone 17 Pro and iPad mini simulator walkthrough opened Review from the
tab bar, set both ratings, changed one, cleared one and reopened the journal.
It also set energy at the largest accessibility text size, checked both
selected values with VoiceOver and cleared the last rating. A separate
database read confirmed empty ratings on both devices and preserved journal
text on iPad. A store test held the first write open, edited the second rating
and switched to yesterday, then verified both values had reached today.

Both signed packages passed validation and reached **VALID** and
**IN_BETA_TESTING** for the existing internal groups. English and Simplified
Chinese testing notes were read back. The builds expire on **2027-02-19**.
