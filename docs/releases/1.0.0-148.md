# Lorvex 1.0.0 (148)

Available on internal TestFlight from [source commit ad991d6a](https://github.com/boyugou/lorvex/commit/ad991d6a02fb1d1a5ed41ee383ade63da67082f0).

## Morning and evening routines

In Today on Mac, iPhone or iPad, choose **Set up routines**, select morning
or evening, and add the habits in the order you do them. Set the start time
to match your day. The steps open for two hours after the start while there
is something left to do. The heading opens or folds them at any time. The
menu lets you switch routines or change the time and order.

A tap checks in a habit. For a counted habit, each tap adds one toward its
daily target. Use its menu to take one back or to skip an untouched habit.
A skipped step is excused in the daily review. A habit in both routines
shares the same daily tally. Habits outside a routine remain available in
Habits and in the iPhone habit grid.

When iCloud sync is enabled, the routine order and start times travel with
your habits. The first sync after upgrading an existing installation reads
the private zone once to recover settings an earlier build may have missed.

## Validation

The native Mac preview exercised setup, reordering, three consecutive row
actions, partial counts, skipping and undo. English and Simplified Chinese
screens were checked in light and dark appearances. The iPhone simulator
showed the morning check-ins on Today. Two independent stores exercised the
production outbound and inbound code with routine preferences arriving
before their referenced habits. Tests checked cadence, the two-hour window,
archiving, restoration, partial counts and excused days against Daily Review.
The full app and core suites, static checks, build metadata tests and localized
argument verification passed.

## Distribution

Apple validated the signed iOS and macOS packages. App Store Connect reported
**VALID** and **IN_BETA_TESTING** for each build. Both are available through
their existing internal tester groups and expire on **2027-02-18**. English
and Simplified Chinese test notes were saved and read back on both platforms.
