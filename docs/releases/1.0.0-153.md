## Today on a smaller screen

On iPad mini in portrait, tasks get the width of the page. Tap **Schedule** to
see events, timed tasks and suggested times. In a wider window the schedule
sits beside the list. At accessibility text sizes, Today keeps one column,
opens long briefings on two lines and puts tasks before the habit routine.
Tap **Show more** for the entire briefing.

An iPad mini simulator walkthrough opened Schedule, rotated to landscape and
continued in the same sheet. Closing it revealed the standing schedule.
The walkthrough reopened it in portrait, opened a timed task, and rotated
with that task still selected. On an empty Today, a separate walkthrough
typed a title, rotated twice, and saved the original draft. At maximum text
size the sample day's first task and its deadline fit in the opening screen.
